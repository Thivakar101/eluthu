# Code of conduct

We want a welcoming, practical place to collaborate. Be respectful, assume good intent, give specific feedback, and make room for people with different experience and backgrounds. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove disruptive comments or contributions and restrict participation when needed. To report conduct concerns, use a private message to repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles. Please include relevant links and context.
